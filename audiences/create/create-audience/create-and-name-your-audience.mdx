---
title: Create and name your audience
description: >-
  Create a new audience from the All Audiences view and give it a unique name on
  the Audience Builder page.
---
1. From the All Audiences view in the Audiences module, click **Create Audience** in the top right corner of the page.
   <Image src="https://docs.sessionm.com/Resources/Images/Procedures/create_audience_2.png" width="624" height="140" alt="All Audiences view with the Create Audience button in the top right corner" caption="" />
2. In the pop-up, provide the audience with a unique name, click create, and you'll be brought to the Audience Builder page.

**Note**: You can always change the name of the audience if needed by editing it at the top of the Audience Builder page and clicking the green checkmark.

<Image src="https://docs.sessionm.com/Resources/Images/Procedures/create_audience_3.png" width="504" height="170" alt="Editing the audience name at the top of the Audience Builder page" caption="" />
