---
title: "Deactivate offer"
description: "Deactivate a Live offer so it is no longer eligible for reward store purchases or as a promotion or loyalty rule outcome."
---

Deactivate an offer. Deactivating an offer makes it no longer eligible for purchases in the rewards store or as an outcome for a promotion or loyalty rule. You can only deactivate an offer that is the Live state.

## Steps

1. On the Offers dashboard, locate the offer that you want to deactivate.
2. Click **Deactivate**.
3. Confirm that you want to deactivate the offer.
