---
title: "Delete offer"
description: "Delete a Draft offer from the SessionM platform. Offers already in customer wallets are unaffected."
---

Delete an offer. Deleting an offer removes it from the SessionM platform. Deleting an offer does not impact any offers already in user's wallets. You can only delete an offer that is in the **Draft** state.

## Steps

1. On the Offers dashboard, locate the offer that you want to delete.
2. Click **Delete**.
3. Confirm that you want to delete the offer.
