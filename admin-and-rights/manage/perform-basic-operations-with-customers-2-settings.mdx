From the Customers 2 Settings page, you can perform a few different basic operations:

- [Removing sub-tabs from a tab](#removing-sub-tabs-from-a-tab)
- [Arranging sub-tabs in a tab](#arranging-sub-tabs-in-a-tab)
- [Disabling and enabling page settings](#disabling-and-enabling-page-settings)

## Removing sub-tabs from a tab

Remove sub-components from a parent component. Doing so returns the sub-component to the Available Components section of the page and, where appropriate, removes the sub-component from view in the Customers Module. For example, removing a sub-component of the _headers_ component would remove the corresponding portion in the customer's profile summary data.

### Steps

1. On the Admin & Rights 2.0 dashboard, under Module Configurations, click **Customers2 Settings**. The Customers 2 Settings page opens.
2. Select the component that contains the sub-component you want to remove.
3. Click the red icon associated with the sub-component. The sub-component is removed from the Selected Components table and now appears in the Available Components section of the page.
4. Click **Save** to implement the change in the Customers Module.

## Arranging sub-tabs in a tab

Arrange how sub-components display within a parent component in the Customers Module. Depending on the related layout, the order of the components may be affected in either a left-to-right or a top-down fashion.

### Steps

1. On the Admin & Rights 2.0 dashboard, under Module Configurations, click **Customers2 Settings**. The Customers 2 Settings page opens.
2. Click and drag the sub-component you want to move to its new location.
3. Click **Save** to implement the change in the Customers Module.

## Disabling and enabling page settings

Disable page settings defined on the Customers 2 Settings page.

### Steps

1. On the Admin & Rights 2.0 dashboard, under Module Configurations, click **Customers2 Settings**. The Customers 2 Settings page opens.
2. Click **Disable Page Settings** to disable the settings defined in the Customers 2 Settings page. In lieu of the disabled settings, the system provides a default view for the Customers Module. Note that disabling page settings also clears the Customers 2 Settings page of any fields, which prevent you from adding or removing any components in the Customers Module.
3. Once disabled, you can click **Enable Page Settings** to restore the page settings.
