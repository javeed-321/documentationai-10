---
title: "Activate or deactivate point account"
description: "Activate or deactivate a point account from the Point Accounts tab of the Point Management page."
---

Activate or deactivate a point account.

## Steps

1. On the Point Accounts tab of the Point Management page, locate the point account you want to modify.
2. If you want to activate a point account:
   1. In the Actions column, click **activate** for the account. The Confirm Activation modal displays.
   2. Click **Activate**. The system displays a message that the point account has been activated, and a new option to deactivate the account now displays in the Actions column for the point account.
3. If you want to deactivate the point account:
   1. Click **deactivate** for the account in the Actions column. The Confirm Archive modal displays.
   2. Click **Deactivate**. The system displays a message that the point account has been deactivated, and only the option to activate the account now displays in the Actions column for the point account.
