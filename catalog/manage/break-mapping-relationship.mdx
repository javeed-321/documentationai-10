---
title: "Break mapping relationship"
description: "Break the link between a store item and its corresponding item in the master catalog. Available for disparate store catalogs only."
---

Break an existing link from a store item and its corresponding item in the master catalog.

## About this task

This action is available only for disparate store catalogs.

## Steps

1. On the Store Catalog tab, locate the item you want to unlink.
2. Click **Break mapping relationship**. The "Break link to master catalog" dialog opens.
3. Click **Unlink**. The item's mapping to the master catalog is broken.

If you are done breaking mapping relationships in the master catalog and want the changes to be applied to all platform modules, click **Publish**.
