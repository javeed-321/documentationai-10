Move categories or items (nodes) from one category to another.

## About this task

When moving an item from one category to another, the system gives you the option to either move or copy the item.

## Steps

1. On the Add/Move Nodes tab, locate the category or item that you want to move.
2. Click and drag the category or item you want to relocate.
   - For categories, the Please Confirm dialog opens asking you if you want to move the category. Click the **Move** button to complete the action.
   - For items, the Please Confirm dialog opens asking if you want to move or copy the item. Click either the **Move** or **Copy** button to complete the action.

A success message displays. The category is moved; the item is copied or moved, depending on which action you chose.
