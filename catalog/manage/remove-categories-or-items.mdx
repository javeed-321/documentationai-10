Remove categories or items (nodes) from a category.

## About this task

Categories that contain other categories or items cannot be removed.

## Steps

1. On the Master Catalog tab, locate the category or item you want to remove.
2. Click either the **Edit category attributes** or **Edit item attributes** button, depending on which type of node you want to remove. The corresponding edit dialog opens.
3. Click the **trash icon** in the dialog. A confirmation dialog opens asking you if you want to permanently delete the category or item.
4. Click **Delete**.

A success message displays. The new category or item is removed from the parent category and permanently deleted.
