The **Scrut - Okta (IDP)** integration enables you to sync users and user group data from your Okta account to Scrut. In this guide, we walk you through the process of **connecting your organization’s Okta account with Scrut as an Identity Provider**.

<Callout kind="alert">
  Heads Up!

  If you’re looking to connect Okta as an SSO provider, [see here](/docs/integrating-okta).
</Callout>

## Prerequisites

Before starting the integration, ensure you have the following access and permissions:

### Access

- You must be a Scrut Admin (or Contributor with access to the Integrations module)
- You need Admin access to your organization’s Okta account

### Permissions

Scrut requires the following read-only permissions:

- okta.users.read: Allows Scrut to read existing users’ profiles and credentials
- okta.groups.read: Allows Scrut to read information about groups and their members in your Okta organization
- okta.roles.read: Allows Scrut to read administrative role assignments for users in your Okta organization

To learn more about Okta permissions, [see here](https://developer.okta.com/docs/api/oauth2).

## How To Integrate Okta IDP With Scrut

### Step 1: Fetch Client ID, Client Secret and Domain from Your Okta Account

1. Log in to your Okta Admin Console.
2. Navigate to **Applications → API Service Integration → Add Integration**.

<Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1786431003819-yu6nq8mg82-integrating-okta-idp__02-navigate-to-applications-api-service.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" width="800" height="600" alt="Navigate to Applications → API Service Integration → Add Integration" caption=" " />
3. Search for and choose **Scrut Automation.** Click **Next**.

<Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1786431005834-3m2wztre4qp-integrating-okta-idp__03-search-for-and-choose-scrut-automation-click.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" width="800" height="600" alt="Search for and choose Scrut Automation. Click Next" caption=" " />
4. Click **Install & Authorize**.

<Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1786431007880-x3stoyaloej-integrating-okta-idp__04-click-install-authorize.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" width="800" height="600" alt="Click Install & Authorize" caption=" " />
5. Copy the **Client Secret** to a safe location. It will not be displayed again.

<Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1786431009744-cebg4ni4htm-integrating-okta-idp__05-copy-the-client-secret-to-a-safe-location-it.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" width="800" height="600" alt="Copy the Client Secret to a safe location. It will not be displayed again" caption=" " />
6. Copy the **Client ID** and **Okta Domain**.

<Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1786431011617-smmd6kwsjp9-integrating-okta-idp__06-copy-the-client-id-and-okta-domain.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" width="800" height="600" alt="Copy the Client ID and Okta Domain" caption=" " />

### Step 2: Go to Integrations in Scrut

1. Sign in to Scrut and click **Integrations** on the left navigation panel.
2. Navigate to the **Integrations Library** tab, choose **Identity Providers** in the **Categories** section.
3. Scroll to the **Okta (IDP)** tile and click **Integrate**.

<Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1786431013222-brwrsi50ku-integrating-okta-idp__07-scroll-to-the-okta-idp-tile-and-click.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" width="800" height="600" alt="Scroll to the Okta (IDP) tile and click Integrate" caption=" " />
4. Enter the details you fetched in the previous step:
  - Client ID
  - Client Secret
  - Okta Domain
5. Click **Submit**.

<Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1786431015048-zlfcz5zefs-integrating-okta-idp__08-click-submit.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" width="800" height="600" alt="Click Submit" caption=" " />

Watch for the success toast message and the Connected status flag indicator.
