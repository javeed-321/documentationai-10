---
title: "Impact of Policy Variables on Existing Policies"
description: "Discover how policy variables impact existing policies in your Scrut organization."
---

When enabling policy variables for the first time for your Scrut organization, you will notice a helpful notification on all policy pages that use variables.

To proceed, click the **Fix Now** button to allow Scrut to add variables at the appropriate locations. You must complete this step to publish the policy or export attachments.

**Note:** Once you have completed the above action for all policies, the Fix Now button will no longer be visible.
