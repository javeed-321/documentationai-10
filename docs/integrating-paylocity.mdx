title: "Integrate Paylocity"

---

The **Scrut-Paylocity integration** fetches employee data from your Paylocity account and sends it to Scrut. Upon completing the integration, Scrut will display the fetched data in:

- People module

## Data Collected

Scrut fetches the following data for all your employees from Paylocity:

- Employee name
- Email address
- Joining date
- Exit date (if applicable)
- Department and Role (if available)
- Email of reporting manager (if available)
- Leave data (start date, end date, and status)

## How To Integrate Paylocity With Scrut

1. Sign in to your Scrut account and click **Integrations** on the left navigation panel.
2. Navigate to the **Integrations Library** tab and select **Human Resource Information Systems** from the Categories section.
3. Scroll to the **Paylocity** tile and click **Integrate**.

4. Click **Connect** on the Paylocity integration page.

<Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1786431097273-uk3g73k3u0k-integrating-paylocity__02-click-connect-on-the-paylocity-integration.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" width="800" height="600" alt="Click Connect on the Paylocity integration page" caption=" " />
5. Fill in the following details:
  - **Paylocity Client ID and Client Secret**: To generate your API credentials, email webservices@paylocity.com with the [attached form completed](https://docs.paylocity.com/knowledge/Partner%20Integration/Paylocity%20Web%20Services_Access%20Request.pdf).
  - **Paylocity Company ID**: You can find your Company ID in the upper left-hand corner of your Paylocity portal.
  - **Paylocity Environment:** Select whether your Paylocity environment is Sandbox or Production.
6. Enter the required credentials and click **Submit**.
7. Once your Paylocity account is successfully integrated with Scrut, you will see a connected flag on the screen.

## What Happens Next?

- Employee data will start syncing immediately. Incremental data will be synced every 24 hours.
- Data can also be manually synced by clicking the Sync Now button on the Paylocity integration page in Scrut.
