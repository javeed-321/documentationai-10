Integrating Scrut with ThreatCop saves hundreds of hours by automatically collecting evidence of an employee's security awareness training.

## Integrating ThreatCop

1. From the left navigation menu, Click Integrations.
2. Click on **Integrations Library**.

<Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1786432254206-2o1ggfp3m0k-click-on-integrations-library-8071202a.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" width="800" height="600" alt="Click on Integrations Library" caption=" " />

3. Scroll to *Campaigns & Trainings section* and find *ThreatCop*.

4. Click **Integrate**.

<Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1786431367408-zl1xnmlay6-integrating-threatcop__02-click-integrate.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" width="800" height="600" alt="Click Integrate" caption=" " />

5. The ThreatCop integration page will open.

<Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1786431369254-foolzvkfu8g-integrating-threatcop__03-the-threatcop-integration-page-will-open.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" width="800" height="600" alt="The ThreatCop integration page will open" caption=" " />

Enter your ThreatCop API V2 token in the designated field.

6. To obtain the API V2 token, reach out to the ThreatCop team directly.

7. Paste the API key in the field and click **Submit**.

8. Upon successful integration, you will see the status marked as Connected.

<Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1786431371020-1dp9rsq3twy-integrating-threatcop__04-upon-successful-integration-you-will-see-the.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" width="800" height="600" alt="Upon successful integration, you will see the status marked as Connected" caption=" " />

9. Click on Configure Campaign Scope.
<Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1786431372749-5a54j2lvi83-integrating-threatcop__05-click-on-configure-campaign-scope.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" width="800" height="600" alt="Click on Configure Campaign Scope" caption=" " />

10. Select the campaign for which the data should be synced for employees.
