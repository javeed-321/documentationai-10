Integrating your SpringVerify account with Scrut helps you to automatically collect evidence related to employee background checks for your compliance requirements.

## Integrating SpringVerify

1. From the left navigation panel, select Integrations.
2. Click on **Available**.

<Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1786432252556-duak5dzr19s-click-on-available-08e3709b.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" width="800" height="600" alt="Click on Available" caption=" " />

3. Scroll to the Background Check section and find ***SpringVerify***.

<Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1786432291731-l30bo60kgn8-scroll-to-background-check-section-and-find-4a7a718a.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" width="800" height="600" alt="Scroll to the Background Check section and find SpringVerify" caption=" " />

4. Click **Integrate**. <Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1786431304961-quu6zffcgzh-integrating-springverify__03-click-integrate.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" width="800" height="600" alt="Click Integrate" caption=" " />
5. In *API Token* field, enter your SpringVerify account API Token.

<Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1786431306784-mprktzac2p-integrating-springverify__04-in-api-token-field-enter-your-springverify.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" width="800" height="600" alt="In API Token field, enter your SpringVerify account API Token" caption=" " />

How to obtain an API Token?

Refer to this [**SpringVerify**](https://support.springworks.in/portal/en/kb/articles/how-to-generate-an-api-key-on-springverify-portal) Guide for obtaining an API Token.

6. Click **Submit**. Upon successful integration, you will receive a notification at the top indicating successful integration.

**Info**:

- Background check data syncs every 24 hours.
- Data can also be manually synced by clicking the three dots and choosing the **Sync Now** option.
