The Scrut - ServiceNow integration fetches compliance data from your [ServiceNow developer account](https://developer.servicenow.com/dev.do#!/) and imports it into Scrut. Upon completing the integration, Scrut will display the fetched data in the following modules:

- Evidence Tasks
- Test

## Prerequisites

Before starting the integration, ensure you have the following permissions for your ServiceNow account:

- **Access Level**: You require Admin access to your ServiceNow account.

<Callout kind="info">
  Pro Tip:

  We recommend logging into your ServiceNow account before starting the integration to avoid doing so later.
</Callout>

## How To Integrate Scrut With ServiceNow

### Step 1: Copy Client ID and Client Secret from ServiceNow

1. Log in to your ServiceNow Development dashboard and navigate to **All → System OAuth → Application Registry**.
2. Click on the **New** button to create a new OAuth endpoint.
3. Select **Create an OAuth API endpoint for external clients**.
4. Configure your new OAuth endpoint as needed, and set the Redirect URL based on your Scrut instance region.
  - **India**: https://app.scrut.io/settings/integration/servicenow
  - **US**: https://app.us.scrut.io/settings/integration/servicenow
  - **EU**: https://app.eu.scrut.io/settings/integration/servicenow
  - **Australia**: https://app.au.scrut.io/settings/integration/servicenow
5. This will generate the OAuth Endpoint with the Client ID and Client Secret you need.

<Callout kind="info">
  Pro Tip!

  For step-by-step instructions, refer to [ServiceNow’s documentation](https://www.servicenow.com/docs/bundle/yokohama-platform-security/page/administer/security/task/t_CreateEndpointforExternalClients.html).
</Callout>
6. Copy and save the **Client ID** and **Client Secret**, as you will need them in the next step.

<Callout kind="danger">
  Note:

  The Client Secret will not be redisplayed. So, ensure that you store it in a safe location for easy access later.
</Callout>

### Step 2: Connect Your ServiceNow Account in Scrut

1. [Sign in to your Scrut account](https://app.scrut.io/) and click **Integrations** on the left navigation panel.

<Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1786432297094-rk4vqrq4ip-sign-in-to-your-scrut-account-and-click-5cb4189e.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" width="800" height="600" alt="Sign in to your Scrut account and click Integrations on the left navigation panel" caption=" " />
2. Navigate to the **Integrations Library** tab and choose **Project Management Platforms** in the **Categories** section.
3. Scroll to the **ServiceNow** tile and click **Integrate**.

<Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1786431238779-hz65zis4yom-integrating-servicenow__02-scroll-to-the-servicenow-tile-and-click.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" width="800" height="600" alt="Scroll to the ServiceNow tile and click Integrate" caption=" " />
4. On the ServiceNow integration page, enter your ServiceNow **Instance Name**.

<Callout kind="alert">
  Heads Up!

  ServiceNow instances follow this naming convention: https://&lt;INSTANCE_NAME>.service-now.com, where &lt;INSTANCE_NAME> is your ServiceNow Instance Name.
</Callout>
5. Paste the **Client ID** and **Client Secret** from Step 1.
6. Click **Submit**.

<Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1786431240762-o26p8n61xnh-integrating-servicenow__03-click-submit.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" width="800" height="600" alt="Click Submit" caption=" " />
7. Click **Authorize** to grant the required permissions.
8. Once authorization is done, you’ll see the “**Connected Successfully**” message and the **Connected** status indicator.

## Data Fetched from ServiceNow

Scrut fetches the following data points from ServiceNow:

- **List of Users**: Username, Name, Email, Title, Department, Active, MFA Enabled, Created
- **List of Groups**: Name, Description, Active, Manager, Parent, Updated
- **List of Incidents**: number, opened_at, short_description, caller_id.value, priority, state, category, assignment_group.value, assigned_to.value, sys_updated_on, sys_updated_by
- **List of Tasks/Issues**: active, assigned_to, sys_created_on, short_description, escalation number, priority, state

<Callout kind="alert">
  Note:

  Refer to ServiceNow’s documentation for more information on the [task table fields](https://www.servicenow.com/docs/bundle/washingtondc-application-development/page/administer/task-table/reference/r_ImportantTaskTableFields.html).
</Callout>

If you encounter any difficulties connecting ServiceNow with Scrut, please get in touch with the Scrut support team at support@scrut.io.
