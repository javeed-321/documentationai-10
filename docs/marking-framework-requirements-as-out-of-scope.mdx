<Callout kind="info">
  Who can use this feature

  - Supported on **Foundation, Growth,** and **Scale** plans
</Callout>

Empower your organization to efficiently identify and categorize compliance framework requirements that aren't applicable, allowing you to sharpen your focus on the essentials.

## How To Mark a Requirement as Out of Scope

1. [Sign in to Scrut](https://app.scrut.io/) and go to **Compliance → Framework** using the left side panel.
2. Click the **My Frameworks** tab, and select the framework for which you want to mark a requirement as “**Out of Scope**.”

<Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1786432263304-bc2w0aseyj-click-the-my-frameworks-tab-and-select-the-84694229.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" width="800" height="600" alt="Click the My Frameworks tab, and select the framework for which you want to mark a requirement as “Out of" caption=" " />
3. Click on the requirement category and click the **three-dot icon** next to the requirement name.

<Callout kind="danger">
  Heads Up!

  You cannot mark a mandatory requirement as Out of Scope.
</Callout>
4. Click **Mark Out Of Scope**.

<Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1786429460734-987mg3n4f35-marking-framework-requirements-as-out-of-scope__02-click-mark-out-of-scope.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" width="800" height="600" alt="Click Mark Out Of Scope" caption=" " />
5. Provide a valid justification for marking the requirement as out of scope and click **Save**.

<Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1786429462970-wbjnqqxbho-marking-framework-requirements-as-out-of-scope__03-provide-a-valid-justification-for-marking.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" width="800" height="600" alt="Provide a valid justification for marking the requirement as out of scope and click Save" caption=" " />
6. Watch out for the confirmation message.

<Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1786429465249-fo58l3r2qrc-marking-framework-requirements-as-out-of-scope__04-watch-out-for-the-confirmation-message.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" width="800" height="600" alt="Watch out for the confirmation message" caption=" " />
