**By default, policies are autosaved as you work in the** [**policy editor**](/docs/how-to-use-policy-editor)**.**

However, you can turn off autosave to manually save your changes. This gives you greater control over when your edits are committed and captured in the revision history.

## How To Turn Off Autosave

<Callout kind="danger">
  Important:

  If you turn off autosave, unsaved changes will be lost unless you manually click the Save button in the policy editor.
</Callout>

1. Navigate to **Compliance → Policies → Settings**.
2. In the **Configurations** tab, disable the **Policy Autosave** toggle.

## Using Manual Save Mode

1. Open your preferred policy, and click **Edit Policy** to view the inline policy editor.

<Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1786432050654-bbup8i9szbt-policy-autosave__04-open-your-preferred-policy-and-click-edit.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" width="800" height="600" alt="Open your preferred policy, and click Edit Policy to view the inline policy editor" caption=" " />
2. As you make edits, you’ll see two buttons: **Save** and **Cancel**.
3. The “**Unsaved Changes**” tip shows that you have unsaved changes.

<Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1786432052252-090n3opx7y7d-policy-autosave__05-the-unsaved-changes-tip-shows-that-you-have.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" width="800" height="600" alt="The “Unsaved Changes” tip shows that you have unsaved changes" caption=" " />
4. **To Save:**
  - Click the **Save** button
  - Your changes are permanently saved
  - A new revision history entry will be created
  - Click the revision history button <Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1786432053919-wx9lon3xqds-policy-autosave__06-screenshot-2025-11-19-at-17-11-44.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" width="20" height="20" alt="Screenshot 2025 11 19 at 17.11.44" caption=" " /> in the toolbar and select **Open Revision History** to view the entry added
  - Click **Back to Editing** to go back to the Policy Editor

<Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1786432055757-7d4w4qda7t4-policy-autosave__07-click-back-to-editing-to-go-back-to-the.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" width="800" height="600" alt="Click Back to Editing to go back to the Policy Editor" caption=" " />
5. **To Cancel:**
  - Click the **Cancel** button
  - A confirmation dialog will appear asking if you want to leave without saving changes
  - If you confirm, all unsaved changes will be lost
  - No revision history entry will be created

### FAQs & Troubleshooting

<ExpandableGroup>

<Expandable title="1: Can multiple users edit a policy simultaneously in the manual save mode?" default-open="false">

No. Only one user can edit a policy at a time. If another user is editing, you'll need to wait until they finish.

</Expandable>

<Expandable title="2: What happens when I switch between the autosave and manual modes?" default-open="false">

**Switching from AutoSave to Manual:**

- Your existing revision history remains unchanged
- Future edits will be captured only when you click the Save button

**Switching from Manual to AutoSave:**

- Your existing revision history remains unchanged
- Future edits will be captured automatically

</Expandable>

<Expandable title="3: What happens to my old revision history when I switch modes?" default-open="false">

Nothing changes. All existing revision history remains intact regardless of mode switches

</Expandable>

<Expandable title="4: How does revision history work in each mode?" default-open="false">

**Manual Save Mode**

- Revision history is created only when you click "Save."
- Each save creates a single history entry that includes all your changes for that session.

**AutoSave Mode:**

- Revision history is created automatically as you edit.
- Multiple history entries may be created during editing.

</Expandable>

<Expandable title="5: When to use manual save mode?" default-open="false">

It’s preferable to enable the manual mode when you want to review all changes before committing them. However, make sure to save your work periodically to avoid losing progress.

</Expandable>

<Expandable title="6: Can I recover changes if I accidentally clicked Cancel?" default-open="false">

No. Changes are permanently discarded when you confirm cancellation.

</Expandable>

</ExpandableGroup>
