## Linking Artifacts to a Control

Controls help keep your organization on track by reducing risks. For controls, you’ll need to provide proof of compliance. In the Scrut platform, these evidentiary materials are referred to as **Artifacts**, which encompass **Policies, Evidence, and Tests**.

To link artifacts to a control, follow these steps:

1. [Sign in to Scrut](https://app.scrut.io/), and click **Compliance → Controls** on the left navigation panel.
2. Go to the **All Controls** tab and select the control you want to link artifacts to.
3. Optionally, use the search bar at the top to quickly locate a specific control. Search by the control name or code.

4. Scroll to the **Control Artifacts** tab and click the **Link Control Artifacts** button.

<Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1786429429287-mohfod0nc8r-linking-controls-and-artifacts__02-scroll-to-the-control-artifacts-tab-and.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" width="800" height="600" alt="Scroll to the Control Artifacts tab and click the Link Control Artifacts button" caption=" " />
5. First, choose the type of artifact you want to link. You can **link all three types - Policy, Evidence, and Test - to a control**.

<Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1786429431512-93gexiv6rbv-linking-controls-and-artifacts__03-first-choose-the-type-of-artifact-you-want.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" width="800" height="600" alt="First, choose the type of artifact you want to link" caption=" " />
6. Select the checkbox or checkboxes that correspond to the artifact or artifacts you want to link.
7. Optionally, use the search bar at the top to narrow the list of artifacts.
8. Click **Link Artifact**.

## Unlinking Artifacts to a Control

To unlink an artifact from a control, follow these steps:

1. [Sign in to Scrut](https://app.scrut.io/), and click **Compliance → Controls** on the left navigation panel.
2. Go to the **All Controls** tab and select the control you want to link artifacts to.
3. Optionally, use the search bar at the top to quickly locate a specific control. Search by the control name or code.

<Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1786432286605-a5lw5ezwdb-optionally-use-the-search-bar-at-the-top-to-83527aac.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" width="800" height="600" alt="Optionally, use the search bar at the top to quickly locate a specific control" caption=" " />
4. Scroll to the **Control Artifacts** tab and click the **Link Control Artifacts** button.

<Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1786429429287-mohfod0nc8r-linking-controls-and-artifacts__02-scroll-to-the-control-artifacts-tab-and.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" width="800" height="600" alt="Scroll to the Control Artifacts tab and click the Link Control Artifacts button" caption=" " />
5. De-select the checkbox or checkboxes that correspond with the artifact or artifacts you want to unlink.
6. Click **Link Artifacts**.
