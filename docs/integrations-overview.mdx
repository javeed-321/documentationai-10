The **Integrations** module serves as the central hub for connecting your tech stack to Scrut. By leveraging these powerful integrations, your organization can automate evidence collection, streamline compliance workflows, and maintain real-time visibility across your entire infrastructure. Additionally, integrations eliminate manual data entry by automatically syncing information from your tools into Scrut, saving time, reducing errors, and ensuring your evidence is always up to date.

Integrations Module

The Integrations module is organized into **Categories**, allowing you to quickly find and configure the connections most relevant to your compliance needs. Each integration card displays the current configuration status, allowing you to monitor your connected applications at a glance. On this page, you can find all the integrations available in Scrut.
