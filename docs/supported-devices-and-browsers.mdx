## Supported Devices

The Scrut platform is designed for use on desktop and laptop computers. For the best experience, use an up-to-date version of your browser.

Mobile and tablet devices (including iPads, Samsung Galaxy tablets, and similar devices) are not supported. If you try to access Scrut on a mobile or tablet device, you will see a message prompting you to switch to a desktop for full functionality.

<Callout kind="alert">
  Heads Up!

  The Scrut Trust Portal is an exception. External stakeholders and team members can view the Trust Portal from mobile and tablet devices. If you are sharing your Trust Portal with stakeholders who may access it on mobile, they can do so without any limitation.
</Callout>

Reach out to [support@scrut.io](mailto:support@scrut.io) or contact your CSM for further assistance.
