---

<Callout kind="info">
  Who can use this feature

  - Supported on **Foundation, Growth,** and **Scale** plans
</Callout>

Scrut offers an extensive library of 70+ policies, pre-mapped with various standard requirements such as ISO, SOC2, GDPR and more. For tailored needs, you also have the flexibility to craft custom policies, accommodating unique organizational requirements.

## How To Create a Custom Policy

1. Sign in to Scrut, click **Compliance → Policies** on the left navigation panel.
2. Click the **Add Policy** button at the top right of the page.

<Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1786431853876-hvyvui3dj2l-creating-policy__01-click-the-add-policy-button-at-the-top-right.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" width="800" height="600" alt="Click the Add Policy button at the top right of the page" caption=" " />
3. Enter policy details, such as:
  - **Name:** Enter a suitable name for the policy.
  - **Policy Requirement:** Optionally, enter a description of the policy.
  - **Assignee:** Use the dropdown to select one or more users who will be responsible for managing the policy in Scrut.

<Callout kind="alert">
  Note:

  The assignee(s) will be notified via email about the assigned policy.
</Callout>
  - **Recurrence:** Select the policy's frequency. For example, if the policy needs to be reviewed quarterly, select Quarterly.
  - **Department:** Select the department to which the policy belongs.
  - **Entity:** Select the organizational entities to which the policy belongs. By default, all policies are linked to “Organization Wide.”
  - **Effort Estimate:** Optionally, you can also select the effort required to implement this policy. You can classify it as **Low, Medium, or High**.
4. Click **Next**.

<Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1786431855661-pe1por26gj8-creating-policy__02-click-next.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" width="800" height="600" alt="Click Next" caption=" " />
5. The next step is to link the controls for the policy.
  - Use the **Frameworks** panel to view controls relevant to your frameworks.
  - Expand a specific requirement to see all controls associated with it.
    - When you select a requirement, all controls within it will be selected by default.
    - If needed, you can then select/unselect specific controls as required.
  - Select the checkboxes next to the controls you want to link.
  - **Note:** Any not applicable requirement will appear grayed out.
  - Optionally, use the search bar at the top to quickly locate controls by name or code.
  - Once you've made the selections, enable the "Show Linked Controls" toggle to review and verify the controls you've chosen to link.
  - Click **Next** to confirm your selections.

<Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1786431857322-jfn9qsijs1o-creating-policy__03-click-next-to-confirm-your-selections.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" width="800" height="600" alt="Click Next to confirm your selections" caption=" " />
6. Review the information and click **Create**.

<Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1786431859028-qx5pjqa9je8-creating-policy__04-review-the-information-and-click-create.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" width="800" height="600" alt="Review the information and click Create" caption=" " />
  - Check the details from the previous steps. To make changes, click **Back**.
  - If everything is correct, click **Create** to finalize your custom policy.

Scrut will create the custom policy.

<Callout kind="alert">
  Heads Up!

  The **Source** column on the **All Policies** table for this policy will be marked as **Custom**. This is to easily differentiate custom policies from those provided by Scrut.
</Callout>
