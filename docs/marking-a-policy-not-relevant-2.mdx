---
title: "Add Policy Assignees"
description: "Learn how to add assignees to policies in Scrut."
---

<Callout kind="info">
  Who can use this feature

  - Supported on **Foundation, Growth,** and **Scale** plans
</Callout>

When creating a custom policy, you can easily add assignees during policy creation. For default Scrut-provided policies, you have two different ways to add assignees:

- [For a Single Policy](/docs/marking-a-policy-not-relevant-2#add-assignees-to-a-single-policy)
- [For Multiple Policies](/docs/marking-a-policy-not-relevant-2#add-assignees-for-multiple-policies)

## Add Assignees to a Single Policy

1. Sign in to Scrut, and click **Compliance → Policies** on the left navigation panel.
2. Go to the **All Policies** tab and select the policy to which you want to add an assignee.

<Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1786432272213-v6h6fiaq9t-go-to-the-all-policies-tab-and-select-the-aea0c51a.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" width="800" height="600" alt="Go to the All Policies tab and select the policy to which you want to add an assignee" caption=" " />
3. Click the **+** icon under the **Assigned to** section.

<Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1786432031277-k337zu5120e-marking-a-policy-not-relevant-2__02-click-the-icon-under-the-assigned-to-section.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" width="800" height="600" alt="Click the + icon under the Assigned to section" caption=" " />
4. Choose the required assignees from the dropdown.
5. To designate a primary assignee, click the **Make Primary** option next to the desired name.
6. Add as many assignees as required.
7. Click **Save** to finalize the assignments.

<Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1786432033227-17facwfshdph-marking-a-policy-not-relevant-2__03-click-save-to-finalize-the-assignments.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" width="800" height="600" alt="Click Save to finalize the assignments" caption=" " />

<Callout kind="tip">
  Heads Up!

  Once added, Scrut will notify all assignees via email regarding their new policy assignment.
</Callout>

## Add Assignees for Multiple Policies

Scrut also gives you the flexibility to add assignees to several policies at once:

1. Go to the **All Policies** tab and select the policies that require assignees.
2. Click the **Edit** button at the top of the Policies table.

<Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1786432034828-28gj47nigu2-marking-a-policy-not-relevant-2__04-click-the-edit-button-at-the-top-of-the.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" width="800" height="600" alt="Click the Edit button at the top of the Policies table" caption=" " />
3. Click the **+** icon next to the **Assignees** field.
4. Choose the required assignees from the dropdown.
5. To designate a primary assignee, click the **Make Primary** option next to the desired name.
6. Add as many assignees as required.
7. Click **Save** to complete the bulk assignments.

<Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1786432036603-hv9vl42ppew-marking-a-policy-not-relevant-2__05-click-save-to-complete-the-bulk-assignments.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" width="800" height="600" alt="Click Save to complete the bulk assignments" caption=" " />

<Callout kind="alert">
  Note:

  If you wish to retain the existing values for fields like Department, Recurrence, Entities, and Relevance, simply leave them unchanged. Scrut will only update the fields you've modified in this window, ensuring that the other values remain intact.
</Callout>
