The **Scrut**-**Keka integration** fetches employee data from your Keka account and syncs it with Scrut. In this guide, we’ll walk you through the step-by-step process of connecting your Keka account with Scrut.

## Prerequisites

Before starting the integration, ensure you have the following access and permissions:

### Account Requirements

- You must be a Scrut Admin (or Contributor with access to the Integration module)
- You must be a Global Admin or IT Admin for your organization’s Keka account

### Permissions

Scrut requires read access to the following data:

- Read: user_profile
- Read: employment_info
- Read: manager_info (wherever applicable)
- Read: leave_data

<Callout kind="info">
  Pro Tip!

  We recommend logging into your organization’s Keka account before starting the integration to avoid doing so later.
</Callout>

## How To Integrate Keka With Scrut

### Step 1: Fetch the API Key, Client ID, and Client Secret from Keka

1. Sign in to your organization’s Keka account, and click the **gear** icon next to your organization's name.
2. Click **Integrations and Automation** on the left panel, then go to the **API Access** tab.
3. **Copy the** **Client ID** and **Client Secret** displayed on the screen. Save these in a safe location, as they won’t be shown again.
4. Click the **Create Key** button.
5. In the Create API Key window, enter the key **Name** (such as Scrut) and select the **Expiry Date**.
6. **Copy the Key** and save it in a safe location, as it will not be visible again once you close this window.
7. In the **Select Scope** section, select:
  - Employee And Org Information
  - Leave
8. Click **Save**.

### Step 2: Go To Integrations in Scrut

1. Sign in to your Scrut account and click **Integrations** on the left navigation panel.
2. Navigate to the **Integrations Library** tab and select **Human Resource Information Systems** from the Categories section.
3. Scroll to the **Keka** tile and click **Integrate**.

## Synchronization Details

- **Initial Sync**: Upon completing the integration, Scrut will begin syncing employee data from your Keka account. Refer to the Audit Log to check sync status.
- **Recurring Sync**: After this, Scrut performs the sync **every 24 hours** to fetch incremental data.
- **Manual Sync:** You can also initiate a manual sync by clicking the **Sync Now** button on the Keka integrations page.

## Data Fetched from Keka

Scrut fetches the following data for all your employees from Keka:

- Employee name
- Email address
- Joining date
- Exit date (if applicable)
- Department and Role (if available)
- Email of reporting manager (if available)
- Leave data (start date, end date, and status)

## Viewing Keka Data in Scrut

To view Keka data in Scrut:

1. Go to **People → Employees → All Employees**.
2. Click on any employee to view their details, including name, email, joining date, reporting manager, and more.
