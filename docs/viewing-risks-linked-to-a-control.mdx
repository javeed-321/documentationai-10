The Controls detail page includes a **Risks tab** that shows all risks mapped to a control, giving you visibility into risk context without leaving the Controls module. This tab lists every risk linked to the control.

## How to View Risks Linked to a Control

1. Navigate to **Compliance → Controls**.
2. Click the control you want to review.
3. Click the **Risks** tab on the control detail page.
4. To filter the list, use the Framework or Entities filters.
5. Click a risk name to open its detail page.

### Points to note

- You can open a risk’s detail page only if you have access to the Risk module. If you don’t have access, the risk name is visible but not clickable.
- The Risks tab is a read-only view of linked risks. You cannot create new risks or link and unlink risks from here.
