## Edit a Control

1. [Log in to Scrut](https://app.scrut.io/), expand the **Compliance** menu on the left navigation panel, and click **Controls**.
2. Click **All Controls** and click the control you wish to modify. Use the search bar at the top to quickly find a specific control.

<Callout kind="info">
  Pro Tip!

  You can search a control by name or code.
</Callout>

## Edit Multiple Controls

Rather than modifying controls individually, you can select multiple controls at once and perform bulk updates or deletions.

1. Navigate to **Compliance → Controls → All Controls** and select the ones you want to modify. You can select multiple controls at once.
2. After selecting the controls, click the consolidated **Edit** icon at the top of the table.
3. This allows you to update key fields for the selected controls, such as:
  - **Assignee**: Reassign the controls to different team members
  - **Control Domain**: Change the control domain
  - **Function Grouping**: Update the function grouping of the controls
  - **Entities**: Modify which entities are associated with the controls
  - **Control Scope**: Set controls in or out of scope
4. Click **Save**.

## Mark a Control Out of Scope

1. Navigate to the **Controls → All Controls** dashboard and click on a specific control.
2. Click the three-dots <Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1786432302475-crxjzp6lqos-three-dots-icon-51c8d982.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" width="20" height="20" alt="Three dots icon" caption=" " /> icon on the top-right and select **Mark Out of Scope**.

<Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1786429255409-rocipqwiisj-editing-and-deleting-control__06-click-the-three-dots-icon-on-the-top-right.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" width="800" height="600" alt="Click the three-dots icon on the top-right and select Mark Out of Scope" caption=" " />
3. Enter your justification for marking the control out of scope.

<Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1786429264766-9evolhtit0d-editing-and-deleting-control__12-enter-your-justification-for-marking-the.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" width="800" height="600" alt="Enter your justification for marking the control out of scope" caption=" " />

<Callout kind="alert">
  Note:

  It’s mandatory to provide a justification to mark a control out of scope.
</Callout>

Watch out for the success notification, “Control marked out of scope successfully!”

### Key Points:

- Once you mark a control out of scope, Scrut updates its status to **Not Applicable**.
- The justification you provide will be visible on the control details page.

<Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1786429266997-we3zcnl87we-editing-and-deleting-control__13-the-justification-you-provide-will-be.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" width="800" height="600" alt="The justification you provide will be visible on the control details page" caption=" " />
- To reverse the action, click the three-dot icons and select **Mark In Scope**. Scrut will remove the previously added justification and show the success message, “Control marked in scope successfully!”
- The audit log captures all scope changes.

<Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1786429269269-262jup0ackv-editing-and-deleting-control__14-the-audit-log-captures-all-scope-changes.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" width="800" height="600" alt="The audit log captures all scope changes" caption=" " />
