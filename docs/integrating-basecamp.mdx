title: "Integrate Basecamp"
---

The Basecamp integration enables Scrut to monitor project-level permissions and user roles within your workspaces. It tracks user activity, project access changes, and collaboration settings to ensure compliance with data access policies.

## Integrating Basecamp

1.From the left navigation panel, select Integrations.

2. Click on **Integrations Library**.

<Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1786432254206-2o1ggfp3m0k-click-on-integrations-library-8071202a.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" width="800" height="600" alt="Click on Integrations Library" caption=" " />

3. Scroll to Project Management Platforms section and find Basecamp.
4. Click **Integrate**.

<Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1786430176209-22bxr9um8h3-integrating-basecamp__02-click-integrate.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" width="800" height="600" alt="Click Integrate" caption=" " />

5. Click **Connect**.

<Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1786430178124-zn2dxr7ifjf-integrating-basecamp__03-click-connect.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" width="800" height="600" alt="Click Connect" caption=" " />

6. Enter your Basecamp account email to authenticate the login.

<Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1786430179725-mjvjlvp7u2r-integrating-basecamp__04-enter-your-basecamp-account-email-to.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" width="800" height="600" alt="Enter your Basecamp account email to authenticate the login" caption=" " />

Allow access for Scrut automation to the Basecamp account.

<Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1786430181423-pn238senrdb-integrating-basecamp__05-allow-access-for-scrut-automation-to-the.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" width="800" height="600" alt="Allow access for Scrut automation to the Basecamp account" caption=" " />

7. You will be redirected to the integration page and there will be a connected flag on successful integration.

<Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1786430183246-fi83gtglj7c-integrating-basecamp__06-you-will-be-redirected-to-the-integration.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" width="800" height="600" alt="You will be redirected to the integration page and there will be a connected flag on successful integration" caption=" " />

### Using the Integration

* After successful integration, you will see different accounts displayed in the Scope Configuration section. Each account represents a unique workspace.

<Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1786430186315-9qujyv6me7-integrating-basecamp__07-after-successful-integration-you-will-see.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" width="800" height="600" alt="After successful integration, you will see different accounts displayed in the Scope Configuration section" caption=" " />

* Under each account, you will find a list of projects available for scope selection.

<Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1786430187985-bz5ainsw936-integrating-basecamp__08-under-each-account-you-will-find-a-list-of.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" width="800" height="600" alt="Under each account, you will find a list of projects available for scope selection" caption=" " />

* To utilize this integration, navigate to any compliance task (e.g., Policies, Evidence, Risk Management or Tests).

* Open any policy and click on the three dots located at the top-right corner.

<Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1786430189837-l4w4itkg62a-integrating-basecamp__09-open-any-policy-and-click-on-the-three-dots.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" width="800" height="600" alt="Open any policy and click on the three dots located at the top-right corner" caption=" " />

* Similar to other integrations, you can create a reference by selecting a project from the list.
<Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1786430191763-q5c09pl37h-integrating-basecamp__10-similar-to-other-integrations-you-can-create.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" width="800" height="600" alt="Similar to other integrations, you can create a reference by selecting a project from the list" caption=" " />

* While creating a task, you will need to provide a Title and Description (both required fields).

* Once these steps are completed, a task is automatically created in the Basecamp account.
