  - Supported on **Foundation, Growth,** and **Scale** plans
</Callout>

## How to Export Controls

1. Sign in to Scrut, and click **Compliance → Controls** on the left navigation panel.
2. Go to the **All Controls** tab.

<Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1786429299235-1ghvyx2lso5-exporting-controls__01-go-to-the-all-controls-tab.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" width="800" height="600" alt="Go to the All Controls tab" caption=" " />
3. Select the controls that you want to export, and click **Export**.

<Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1786429301385-o9t8kidwr1a-exporting-controls__02-select-the-controls-that-you-want-to-export.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" width="800" height="600" alt="Select the controls that you want to export, and click Export" caption=" " />
4. Scrut will automatically download the selected controls.

<Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1786429303640-62ewz1czvvl-exporting-controls__03-scrut-will-automatically-download-the.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" width="800" height="600" alt="Scrut will automatically download the selected controls" caption=" " />
