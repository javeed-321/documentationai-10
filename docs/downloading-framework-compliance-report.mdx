<Callout kind="info">
  Who can use this feature

  - Supported on **Foundation, Growth,** and **Scale** plans
</Callout>

The **Framework Compliance Report** helps to identify and address non-compliant elements within specific frameworks. This report can be shared with your organization's leadership team, auditors, and other stakeholders.

## How To Download Framework Compliance Report

Users can download a framework-level report in two ways:

### Method 1: Via the Framework Detail Page

Open the framework detail page, click the three-dots icon on the top right, and select **Export Framework Compliance Report**.

Select the options to include in the report and click **Download**. Scrut will email the report to you.

<Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1786429236772-f3hmoescrww-downloading-framework-compliance-report__02-select-the-options-to-include-in-the-report.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" width="800" height="600" alt="Select the options to include in the report and click Download. Scrut will email the report to you" caption=" " />

### Method 2: Via the Reports Module

Click **Reports** on the left navigation panel. Click the **Download** button on the Framework Compliance report.

<Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1786429239349-noxb5s57j4-downloading-framework-compliance-report__03-click-reports-on-the-left-navigation-panel.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" width="800" height="600" alt="Click Reports on the left navigation panel. Click the Download button on the Framework Compliance report" caption=" " />

Choose the Framework, select the options, and click **Download**. Scrut will email the report to you.

<Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1786429241994-neqwjor6yx-downloading-framework-compliance-report__04-choose-the-framework-select-the-options-and.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" width="800" height="600" alt="Choose the Framework, select the options, and click Download. Scrut will email the report to you" caption=" " />

<Callout kind="alert">
  Note:

  Once you request the report, Scrut will share the link to download the report to your email. This link will **expire in 7 days**.
</Callout>
