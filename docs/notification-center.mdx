The **Notification Center** in Scrut helps you keep track of important updates in one single place. You can quickly see what needs attention and take action.

## How To Access the Notification Center

Once you’re signed in to Scrut, click the **bell icon** <Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1786429599066-dk9x9j76g-notification-center__01-notification-bell.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" width="20" height="20" alt="Notification Bell" caption=" " /> in the top right next to your profile name. The red badge shows the number of unread notifications.

### Points To Know

- You’ll see notifications about billing updates, platform maintenance, integration updates, product announcements, etc.
- Notifications are ordered by priority. The most important ones always appear first. Others are sorted from newest to oldest. Critical notifications (like billing or downtime) remain visible until addressed.
- Notifications include CTAs that take you directly to the corresponding modules in Scrut.
- Click the circle to the right of a notification to mark it as read.
- Use the “Show Only Unread” toggle to focus on new items.
- Notifications are unique to your account and role, so you’ll only see what’s relevant to you.
