To populate the vulnerabilities, Scrut needs to be integrated with Snyk.

## How To Integrate Snyk with Scrut

1. [Sign in to Scrut](https://app.scrut.io/) and click **Integrations** on the left navigation panel.
2. Navigate to the **Integrations Library** tab and choose **Vulnerability Scanners** in the **Categories** section.
3. Scroll to the **Snyk** tile and click **Integrate**.

<Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1786431266318-wd7298th9zk-integrating-snyk__01-scroll-to-the-snyk-tile-and-click-integrate.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" width="800" height="600" alt="Scroll to the Snyk tile and click Integrate" caption=" " />
4. Open your Snyk account, navigate to Settings, and then select General.
5. Copy the Organization ID.
6. Generate the API Key by clicking on Manage Service Accounts.

<Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1786431268189-b0x6xs6xmr-integrating-snyk__02-generate-the-api-key-by-clicking-on-manage.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" width="800" height="600" alt="Generate the API Key by clicking on Manage Service Accounts" caption=" " />
7. Paste the credentials in your Scrut Integration Page.

<Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1786431270233-lyxysa2xj6-integrating-snyk__03-paste-the-credentials-in-your-scrut.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" width="800" height="600" alt="Paste the credentials in your Scrut Integration Page" caption=" " />

9. Click **Connect**.
10. Integration is successful!
