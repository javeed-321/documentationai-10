title: "Integrate Certn"
---

Certn integration enables users to collect evidence related employee background checks, within the Scrut platform.

## Integrating Certn

1. From the left navigation panel, select Integrations.
2. Click on **Available**.

<Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1786432252556-duak5dzr19s-click-on-available-08e3709b.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" width="800" height="600" alt="Click on Available" caption=" " />

3. Scroll to *Background Check* section and find *Certn*.

<Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1786432291731-l30bo60kgn8-scroll-to-background-check-section-and-find-4a7a718a.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" width="800" height="600" alt="Scroll to Background Check section and find Certn" caption=" " />

4. Click **Integrate**.
<Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1786430252999-jjx8x0z1xm-integrating-certn__03-click-integrate.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" width="800" height="600" alt="Click Integrate" caption=" " />

5. In *API Token* field, enter your Certn account API Token.
<Callout kind="info">
  How to obtain API Token?

  Refer to this [Certn Guide](https://docs.certn.co/api/) for obtaining API Token.
</Callout>
6. Click **Submit**.
Upon successful integration, you will receive a notification at the top indicating that the integration was successful.

<Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1786430254767-jmic67o3kzj-integrating-certn__04-upon-successful-integration-you-will-receive.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" width="800" height="600" alt="Upon successful integration, you will receive a notification at the top indicating that the integration was" caption=" " />

<Callout kind="info">
  **Info**:
  * Background check data syncs every 24 hours.
  * Data can also be manually synced by clicking the three dots and choosing the **Sync Now** option.
</Callout>
