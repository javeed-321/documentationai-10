title: "Integrate KiteCyber"
description: "Learn how to connect your KiteCyber account with Scrut."
---

The KiteCyber integration fetches employees' endpoint/device data to Scrut. This data is displayed in the People Dashboard and the People section's Employee module. Scrut fetches the following data from Kitecyber:

- Device name
- OS version
- Antivirus Installed(Yes/No)
- HD Encrypted(Yes/No)
- Screenlock Enabled(Yes/No)

## Integrating KiteCyber

1. From the left navigation panel, select Integrations.
2. Click on *Available*.

<Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1786430747875-7rvvab1v688-integrating-kitecyber__01-click-on-available.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" width="800" height="600" alt="Click on Available" caption=" " />

3. Scroll to the Mobile Device Management Tools section and find KiteCyber. Optionally, use the search bar to locate the integration.
4. Click on *Integrate*.

<Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1786430749547-w5pk4neje7s-integrating-kitecyber__02-click-on-integrate.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" width="800" height="600" alt="Click on Integrate" caption=" " />

5. Enter the Base URL without the prefix, the Client ID, and the Client Secret, and click on *Submit*.

<Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1786430751152-wqzpt8v3is-integrating-kitecyber__03-enter-the-base-url-without-the-prefix-the.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" width="800" height="600" alt="Enter the Base URL without the prefix, the Client ID, and the Client Secret, and click on Submit" caption=" " />

6. Once done, you will see a connected flag.

<Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1786430752721-94oqplypu4i-integrating-kitecyber__04-once-done-you-will-see-a-connected-flag.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" width="800" height="600" alt="Once done, you will see a connected flag" caption=" " />

## How to retrieve Credentials from KiteCyber

- **Login**: Access KiteCyber using an admin account.
- **API Details**: Navigate to **Settings > API** to retrieve the Client ID. The Client Secret will be sent directly to your email from KiteCyber.
- **Base URL**: Locate the Base URL by going to **Settings > Account > Contact Info**.
