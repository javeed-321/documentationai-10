The **Scrut**-**HiBob integration** fetches employee data from your HiBob account and syncs it with Scrut. In this guide, we’ll walk you through the step-by-step process of connecting your HiBob account with Scrut.

## Prerequisites

Before starting the integration, ensure you have the following access and permissions:

### Account Requirements

- You must be a Scrut Admin (or Contributor with access to the Integration module)
- Permission to [create an API service user](https://apidocs.hibob.com/docs/api-service-users) for your organization’s HiBob account

### Permissions

Scrut requires the following **read-only permissions** to fetch the requisite data from HiBob:

- **employee_profile**: Get profile data of an employee, like first and last names, date of birth, work email, termination date, hire date, etc.
- **employee_orgStructure**: Get hierarchical data related to an employee, like the employee’s manager, department, and role.
- **employee_leave_requests**: Get the leave requests for an employee.

<Callout kind="info">
  Pro Tip!

  We recommend logging into your organization’s HiBob account before starting the integration to avoid doing so later.
</Callout>

## How To Integrate HiBob With Scrut

### Step 1: Fetch Service User ID and Token from HiBob

1. Sign in to your organization’s HiBob account and go to the **Service Users configuration page** to create a new user. and **copy the ID and token**.
2. To access Employee data via the API, you need to **grant basic view access to each category** mentioned in the [permissions section](/docs/integrating-hibob#permissions) above:
  - **View all employees’ [Category name] sections**
3. Once you grant the required permissions, save the changes.
4. **Copy the service user ID and token** in a safe location. You’ll need to enter them in Scrut in the next step.

<Callout kind="info">
  Pro Tip!

  For step-by-step instructions on [creating the service user and assigning the required permissions](https://apidocs.hibob.com/docs/api-service-users), refer to HiBob’s help guide. For more information on the API, [see here](https://apidocs.hibob.com/docs/getting-started).
</Callout>

### Step 2: Navigate to Integrations in Scrut

1. Sign in to your Scrut account and click **Integrations** on the left navigation panel.
2. Navigate to the **Integrations Library** tab and select **Human Resource Information Systems** from the Categories section.
3. Scroll to the **HiBob** tile and click **Integrate**.

4. Click the **Connect** button at the top right of the HiBob integration page.

7. Click **Submit**.

On successful integration, you’ll see the success toast message and the connected flag.

## Synchronization Details

- **Initial Sync**: Upon completing the integration, Scrut will begin syncing employee data from your HiBob account. Refer to the Audit Log to check sync status.
- **Recurring Sync**: After this, Scrut performs the sync **every 24 hours** to fetch incremental data.
- **Manual Sync:** You can also initiate a manual sync by clicking the **Sync Now** button on the HiBob integrations page.

## Data Fetched from HiBob

Scrut fetches the following data for all your employees from HiBob:

- Employee name
- Email address
- Joining date
- Exit date (if applicable)
- Department and Role (if available)
- Email of reporting manager (if available)
- Leave data (start date, end date, and status)

## Viewing HiBob Data in Scrut

To view HiBob data in Scrut:

1. Go to **People → Employees → All Employees**.
2. Click on any employee to view their details, including name, email, joining date, reporting manager, and more.
