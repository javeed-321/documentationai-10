The **Scrut - Trello Integration** allows you to create [Trello](https://trello.com/) tickets directly from Scrut. This integration enhances your workflow by allowing you to create and assign tasks to team members without switching to your Trello environment. This article provides a step-by-step guide for integrating your Trello account with your Scrut account.

## How to Connect Trello With Scrut

### Prerequisites

Before you begin the integration, make sure to have the following permissions for your Trello account:

- **Account information:** This is for Scrut to identify your Trello account details.
- Admin access to Trello
  - **Read access:** To enable Scrut to fetch Trello workspaces, boards, and task details for tracking.
  - **Write access:** To create and update tasks in Trello.

<Callout kind="info">
  Pro Tip!

  We recommend logging into your Trello account before beginning the integration to avoid doing so later.
</Callout>

### Step 1: Navigate to Integrations in Scrut

1. [Log in to your Scrut account](https://app.scrut.io/) and click **Integrations** on the left navigation panel.
2. In the **Categories** section, click **Project Management Platforms**, and click the **Integrations Library** tab on the top. <Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1786431374677-9gz4xi7e90d-integrating-trello__01-trello-integration-12.jpg?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" width="800" height="600" alt="Trello Integration 12" caption=" " />
3. Find the **Trello** card, or use the search bar on the top of the page to find Trello and click the **Integrate** button. <Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1786431376419-jhzaaentpcb-integrating-trello__02-trello-integration-13.jpg?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" width="800" height="600" alt="Trello Integration 13" caption=" " />

### Step 2: Connect Your Trello Account

1. From the Trello integration page, click the **Connect** button on the top-right. <Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1786431378189-2j1kultyza6-integrating-trello__03-trello-integration-14.jpg?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" width="800" height="600" alt="Trello Integration 14" caption=" " />
2. Scrut will redirect you to the Trello authorization page.

<Callout kind="alert">
  Heads Up!

  If you haven’t logged into your Trello account, Scrut will prompt you to login before you can continue.
</Callout>

3. Click **Allow** to complete the authorization process. <Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1786431379834-fp1gbrk0fyi-integrating-trello__04-trello-integration-15.jpg?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" width="800" height="600" alt="Trello Integration 15" caption=" " />

Watch out for the **"Connected Successfully"** message and the **Connected** status indicator. <Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1786431381620-d6397wcnr84-integrating-trello__05-trello-integration-16.jpg?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" width="800" height="600" alt="Trello Integration 16" caption=" " />

### Step 3: Configure the Scope of Integration

1. From the Trello integration page in Scrut, click the **Configure Scope** button on the top right. <Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1786431383587-gdbwann6lxc-integrating-trello__06-trello-integration-17.jpg?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" width="800" height="600" alt="Trello Integration 17" caption=" " />
2. Select your Trello workspace and the boards you want to connect with Scrut. You can connect multiple Trello workspaces/boards as needed. <Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1786431385530-zx17mqpeo79-integrating-trello__07-trello-integration-18.jpg?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" width="800" height="600" alt="Trello Integration 18" caption=" " />
3. Click **Save**.

### Step 4: Create a Trello Ticket

Once you have configured the scope of the integration, you can create a Trello ticket from any compliance task module, such as:

- Policies
- Evidence Tasks
- Risk Management
- Tests

Navigate to your required policy/evidence task/risk/automated test, click the three-dots icon on the top-right of the page, and select **Create Trello Ticket**. <Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1786431387289-5xfp40ezhtb-integrating-trello__08-trello-integration-19.jpg?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" width="800" height="600" alt="Trello Integration 19" caption=" " />

4. Enter the ticket details: Name, Workspace, Board, List, Assignees, and Description.
5. Click **Create**.

## Further Insights

### **1: How long does it take for a ticket created in Scrut to reflect on my Trello board?**

The action is real-time, and the ticket you create will reflect immediately in Trello.

### **2: Can I link multiple Trello workspaces to my Scrut account?**

Yes. Click the **Configure Scope** button on the Trello integration page in Scrut, and select multiple workspaces.

### **3: How do I revoke Scrut's access to my Trello account?**

Click the **Disconnect** button from the Trello integration page to stop sharing data from your Trello account to Scrut. <Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1786431396390-94whqjb9g2-integrating-trello__13-trello-integration-21.jpg?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" width="800" height="600" alt="Trello Integration 21" caption=" " />

### **4: Is the integration bidirectional?**

No. Currently, the integration is unidirectional, meaning data passes from Scrut to Trello and not the other way around.
