---
title: "Scheduled Maintenance: 11-12 July 2026"
description: "Trust Portals will be unavailable temporarily due to scheduled maintenance on 11 and 12 July 2026."
---

Scrut periodically performs scheduled maintenance to keep the platform secure and reliable. As part of the upcoming maintenance, **Scrut-hosted Trust Portals will be temporarily unavailable on July 11 and 12, 2026**, while we apply security patches.

During this time, your Trust Portal will be inaccessible to visitors, including anyone who accesses it through your website, security page, or vendor questionnaires. All other parts of the Scrut platform will continue to work as usual, and your Trust Portal data remains safe and unaffected throughout.

If your Trust Portal is linked in external-facing materials, you may want to give your stakeholders and customers a heads-up about the downtime dates in advance.

## FAQs

<ExpandableGroup>

<Expandable title="1: Will my Trust Portal data be affected?" default-open="false">

No. The maintenance window affects access only. Your Trust Portal content, settings, and data remain intact throughout.

</Expandable>

<Expandable title="2: Do I need to take any action after maintenance is complete?" default-open="false">

No action is required. Your Trust Portal will resume normal availability automatically once the maintenance window closes.

</Expandable>

</ExpandableGroup>
