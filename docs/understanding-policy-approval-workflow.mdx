## What Is Policy Approval Workflow?

Scrut offers an additional feature to add extra approval layers to your policy workflow. Designated assignees or admins can add approvers even after the policy is created, ensuring a structured approval process. Once all necessary approvals are obtained, the policy can be published.

## Multilevel Approval Workflow

Significant policy changes or new procedures often require multiple approvals.

*For example, in a software development firm, a code deployment policy might require approval from the Quality Assurance Lead, the Chief Technology Officer, and finally the Chief Information Security Officer. The policy is published only after all approvers have given their consent, ensuring thorough endorsement from relevant stakeholders.*

## How to Turn On/Off Policy Approval Workflow

Follow these steps to enable or disable the policy approval workflow:

1. Click **Compliance → Policies** on the left navigation panel.
2. Click **Settings** on the top-right.
