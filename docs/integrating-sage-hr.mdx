The **Scrut** **-** **Sage HR integration** seamlessly syncs employee data from your [Sage HR](https://www.sage.com/en-gb/sage-business-cloud/hr/) account to Scrut so that you can easily access crucial employee details in the [**People → Employees module**](https://help.scrut.io/docs/employees) in Scrut. This article provides a step-by-step guide for integrating your Sage HR account with Scrut.

## Prerequisites

Before you begin the integration, make sure to have the following permissions for your Sage HR account:

- **Account Information**: Login credentials
- **Access Level**: Admin access to Sage HR
- **API enabled** for your organization’s Sage HR account
  - To enable the Sage HR API key, navigate to **Profile → Settings → Integrations → API → Enable API Access** in your Sage HR account.
- **Permissions**: Scrut requires read access to the following data:
  - Read: user_profile
  - Read: employment_info
  - Read: manager_info (wherever applicable)
  - Read: leave_dataAccess to your organization’s Sage HR account

<Callout kind="info">
  Pro Tip!

  We recommend logging into your Sage HR account before beginning the integration to avoid doing so later.
</Callout>

## How to Connect Sage HR With Scrut

### Step 1: Navigate to Integrations in Scrut

1. [Log in to Scrut](https://app.scrut.io/login) and click **Integrations** on the left panel.
2. Click the **Integrations Library** tab at the top, and in the **Categories** section, click **Human Resources Information Systems**.
3. Locate the **Sage HR** card, or use the search bar at the top of the page to find it, and click the **Integrate** button.

The employee details fetched from your Sage HR account will now be visible in the [People → Employees](https://help.scrut.io/docs/employees) module in Scrut.

## Synchronization Details

- **Initial Sync**: Upon completing the integration, Scrut will immediately sync employee data from your Sage HR account.
- **Recurring Sync**: After this, Scrut performs the sync **every 24 hours** to fetch incremental data.
- **Manual Sync:** You can also initiate a manual sync by clicking the **Sync Now** button on the Sage HR integrations page.

## Data Fetched From Sage HR

Scrut fetches the following employee details from Sage HR:

- Employee Name (First and Last Name)
- Email Address
- Employment Start Date
- Date of Exit (if applicable)
- Reporting Manager Email
- Start and End Dates of Employee Leaves

## Viewing Sage HR Data in Scrut

To view Sage HR data in Scrut:

1. Go to **People → Employees → All Employees**.
2. Click on any employee to view their details, including name, email, joining date, reporting manager, and more.
