## Creating a Shortcut Story

1. Log into app.scrut.io.
2. Form the left navigation panel expand the compliance menu.

<Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1786429856296-bin63zui4j-creating-a-shortcut-story__01-form-the-left-navigation-panel-expand-the.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" width="800" height="600" alt="Form the left navigation panel expand the compliance menu" caption=" " />

3. Click on **Cloud**

<Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1786429858240-v0q2eq14ci-creating-a-shortcut-story__02-click-on-cloud.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" width="800" height="600" alt="Click on Cloud" caption=" " />

4. Navigate to **Test** section.

<Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1786429860287-j657eoqlra-creating-a-shortcut-story__03-navigate-to-test-section.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" width="800" height="600" alt="Navigate to Test section" caption=" " />

5. After landing on the Test page, open the test corresponding to which you want to create a Linear issue.

<Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1786429862133-sltxgi7hb0c-creating-a-shortcut-story__04-after-landing-on-the-test-page-open-the-test.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" width="800" height="600" alt="After landing on the Test page, open the test corresponding to which you want to create a Linear issue" caption=" " />

6. After opening the respective test, click on the three vertical dots button on the top right corner. 

<Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1786429863872-h3n1y0j3qpw-creating-a-shortcut-story__05-after-opening-the-respective-test-click-on.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" width="800" height="600" alt="After opening the respective test, click on the three vertical dots button on the top right corner" caption=" " />

7. Click on *Create Shortcut Story*.

<Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1786429865840-l456covi8v-creating-a-shortcut-story__06-click-on-create-shortcut-story.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" width="800" height="600" alt="Click on Create Shortcut Story" caption=" " />

8. Once done, you will be prompted to fill in the story fields that you had selected during integration.

<Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1786429867624-p21700sgqr-creating-a-shortcut-story__07-once-done-you-will-be-prompted-to-fill-in.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" width="800" height="600" alt="Once done, you will be prompted to fill in the story fields that you had selected during integration" caption=" " />

9. Once done, click on *Create*.

<Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1786429869385-hzsccztzuc-creating-a-shortcut-story__08-once-done-click-on-create.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" width="800" height="600" alt="Once done, click on Create" caption=" " />
