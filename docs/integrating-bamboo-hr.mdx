---
title: "Integrate Bamboo HR"
description: "Learn how to connect your Bamboo HR account with Scrut to sync employee information."
---

Bamboo HR integration syncs employee data from Bamboo HR to Scrut. This information is then displayed in the **People → Employees** module. Scrut fetches the following employee information from Bamboo HR:

- Employee Name
- Email address
- Joining Date
- Exit Date
- Department
- Reporting Manager

## How To Connect Bamboo HR With Scrut

1. From the left navigation panel, select Integrations.
2. Click on **Available**.

<Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1786430167181-3sak62byi85-integrating-bamboo-hr__01-click-on-available.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" width="800" height="600" alt="Click on Available" caption=" " />

3. Scroll to the *Human Resource Information Systems* section and find *Bamboo HR*.
4. Click **Integrate**.

<Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1786430168972-n1pa0uwcc9-integrating-bamboo-hr__02-click-integrate.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" width="800" height="600" alt="Click Integrate" caption=" " />
5. Click **Connect**.

<Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1786430170868-n99cguzve9r-integrating-bamboo-hr__03-click-connect.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" width="800" height="600" alt="Click Connect" caption=" " />
6. Click **Yes** if you are the admin for Bamboo HR.

<Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1786430172812-aqkxxp1eu5-integrating-bamboo-hr__04-click-yes-if-you-are-the-admin-for-bamboo-hr.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" width="800" height="600" alt="Click Yes if you are the admin for Bamboo HR" caption=" " />

The Bamboo HR credential window opens. <Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1786430174477-rn61wfyk0e-integrating-bamboo-hr__05-the-bamboo-hr-credential-window-opens.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" width="800" height="600" alt="The Bamboo HR credential window opens" caption=" " />

7. In *the Subdomain* field, enter the subdomain. For example, if you sign in at "acme.bamboohr.com", enter "acme".
8. In the API Key field, enter the api key.

How do I fetch the API Key?

To generate an API key, log in and click the name icon in the top right corner of any page on BambooHR to open the *user context menu*. With sufficient permissions, there will be an *API Keys* option in that menu to get the key.

10. Click **Submit**.
11. Once your BambooHR account is successfully integrated with Scrut, you will see a connected flag on the page.

### Points to note

- Employee data will start syncing immediately. Incremental data will be synced every 24 hours.
- Data can also be manually synced by clicking the three dots and choosing the **Sync Now** option.
