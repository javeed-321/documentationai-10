---
title: "Delete a Policy"
description: "Learn how to delete policies in Scrut."
---

<Callout kind="info">
  Who can use this feature

  - Supported on **Foundation, Growth,** and **Scale** plans
</Callout>

To delete one or more policies:

1. Sign in to Scrut, and click **Compliance → Policies** on the left navigation panel.
2. Go to the **All Policies** tab and select the policies you want to delete.
3. You can select one or more policies, as required.
4. Click the **Delete** button at the top of the table.

5. Click Delete to confirm your action.

<Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1786431864156-vhk9mzg8hs-delete-a-policy__02-click-delete-to-confirm-your-action.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" width="800" height="600" alt="Click Delete to confirm your action" caption=" " />

<Callout kind="alert">
  Heads Up!

  Once you delete the policy, it cannot be recovered. Review the selected policies carefully before confirming deletion. If you only want to **delete policy content**, [see here](/docs/removing-policy-content).
</Callout>
