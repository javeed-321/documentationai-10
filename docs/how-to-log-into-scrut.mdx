Congrats on signing up for Scrut! We’re excited to have you on board. **Scrut is a web-based platform that runs in your browser; you don’t need to install any apps or software.**

## For First-Time Users

You would have received an email from Scrut with your unique access link. Click the **Login** button in the email and follow the on-screen instructions.

<Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1786429574184-pdb9m01y0q-how-to-log-into-scrut__01-first-time-user-login-email.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" width="800" height="600" alt="First time user login email" caption=" " />

<Callout kind="tip">
  Heads Up!

  If it’s the first time someone from your organization is logging into Scrut, you'll be directed to [set up your organizational profile and customize your experience](/docs/onboarding-tasks).
</Callout>

## For Returning Users

1. Open the Scrut login page: [**https://app.scrut.io/**](https://app.scrut.io/)
2. Enter your registered email (usually your work email) and click **Continue**.

<Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1786429576439-gcuyt1maq7-how-to-log-into-scrut__02-login-screen-enter-email.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" width="800" height="600" alt="Login Screen - Enter Email" caption=" " />
3. Log in using your preferred SSO option (if enabled for your organization) or click **Get Verification Code** to request a one-time code sent to your email.

<Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1786429578587-gvywfhg0pdo-how-to-log-into-scrut__03-login-screen-sso-and-otp.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" width="800" height="600" alt="Login Screen - SSO and OTP" caption=" " />

That’s it! You can now access Scrut and get started on your compliance journey.
