---
title: "Integrate Heroku"
description: "Learn how to connect your Heroku account with Scrut."
---

1. From the left navigation panel, click **Integrations**. <Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1786430632291-wow2l6u5s7-integrating-heroku__01-from-the-left-navigation-panel-click.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" width="800" height="600" alt="From the left navigation panel, click Integrations" caption=" " />
2. Click **Available**. <Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1786430633928-afmv7podzj9-integrating-heroku__02-click-available.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" width="800" height="600" alt="Click Available" caption=" " />
3. Go to the Cloud Provider section and find Heroku.
4. Click **Integrate**.

<Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1786430635665-psa0i945y3-integrating-heroku__03-click-integrate.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" width="800" height="600" alt="Click Integrate" caption=" " />

5. Click **Connect**. <Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1786430637617-j79gqb0z4nl-integrating-heroku__04-click-connect.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" width="800" height="600" alt="Click Connect" caption=" " />

It will redirect to Heroku's authentication page.

<Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1786430639345-442yold1eyc-integrating-heroku__05-it-will-redirect-to-herokus-authentication.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" width="800" height="600" alt="It will redirect to Heroku's authentication page" caption=" " />

6. Enter the admin account credentials to integrate, and this will redirect to the integration page on successful integration, and a connected flag will be shown on the app.
