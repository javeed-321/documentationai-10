The **Task Center** is your centralized hub for tracking and acting on compliance work across your organization. Instead of hunting through multiple modules to understand what needs attention, the Task Center surfaces everything in a single, organized view.

When you're working toward a compliance audit or completing routine compliance tasks, there are always multiple items that require your attention: evidence to collect, policies to publish, tests to fix, risks to address, and more. The Task Center aggregates all of these into a unified view so you can see it all at a glance, track progress by module, and focus on what's pending.

## Task Center Tabs

The Task Center includes three tabs, each designed for a specific workflow.

### Audit Prep

Use this tab when preparing for a compliance audit. It helps you track audit-related tasks, monitor team-wide progress, and follow up on open items.

### Continuous Compliance

This tab gives you a high-level view of ongoing compliance work across all active modules. Use it to monitor your compliance health, identify bottlenecks, and keep work moving between audits.

### My Tasks

This is your personal task dashboard. It shows only the tasks assigned to you, along with the context you need to complete them.

<Callout kind="info" collapsed="false">
  Not sure which tab to focus on? Here’s a cheat sheet to help you out:

  - **Preparing for an upcoming audit?** Go to [**Audit Prep**](/docs/task-center-audit-prep) to see what’s complete, what’s pending, and who’s responsible across all relevant modules.
  - **Managing year-round compliance?** Use [**Continuous Compliance**](/docs/continuous-compliance-task-center) to track overall progress and follow up with contributors.
  - **Completing your own work?** Open [**My Tasks**](/docs/my-tasks-task-center) to focus on what’s assigned to you, without distractions.
</Callout>
