The **Scrut - Oracle Sales & Fusion Service** integration fetches user access data, permissions, and role assignments from your Oracle Sales & Fusion Service account and sends them to the [Access Review](https://help.scrut.io/docs/what-is-access-review) module. This integration enables your organization to maintain comprehensive visibility over user access rights and streamline periodic access reviews for compliance and security.

<Callout kind="alert">
  Heads Up!

  The data synced from Oracle Sales & Fusion Service flows into the **Access Review** module in Scrut.
</Callout>

## Prerequisites

Before starting the integration, ensure you have the following access and permissions:

### Access

- You must be a **Scrut Admin** (or Contributor with access to the Integrations module)
- You must be an **Oracle Fusion Cloud User with API Access**
- Login credentials and access to your organization’s Oracle Sales & Fusion Service account

### Permissions

Scrut requires **read-only access** to the following data in Oracle Sales & Fusion Service

- User Accounts: View name, email, and user role

<Callout kind="info">
  Pro Tip!

  We recommend logging into your Oracle Sales & Fusion Service account before starting the integration to avoid doing so later.
</Callout>

## How To Connect Oracle Sales & Fusion Service With Scrut

1. [Sign in to Scrut](https://app.scrut.io/) and click **Integrations** on the left navigation panel.
2. Navigate to the **Integrations Library** tab and choose **Customer Relationship Management** in the **Categories** section.
3. Scroll to the **Oracle Sales & Fusion Service** tile and click **Integrate**.

<Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1786431073027-497o0s8zrz7-integrating-oracle-sales-fusion-service__01-scroll-to-the-oracle-sales-fusion-service.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" width="800" height="600" alt="Scroll to the Oracle Sales & Fusion Service tile and click Integrate" caption=" " />
4. Enter the required details: Baseurl, Username, and Password for your Oracle Sales & Fusion Service account.

<Callout kind="info">
  Pro Tip!

  Refer to this help documentation from Oracle on [how to find your baseurl](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/enterprise-performance-management-common/prest/url_structure_overview.html). The &lt;BaseURL> is the first part of your service URL, before the context.

  For example, if your service URL is

  [https://epm-acme.epm.us-phoenix-.ocs.oraclecloud.com/epmcloud,](https://epm-acme.epm.us-phoenix-1.ocs.oraclecloud.com/epmcloud,)

  your &lt;BaseURL> is [https://epm-acme.epm.us-phoenix-1.ocs.oraclecloud.com](https://epm-acme.epm.us-phoenix-1.ocs.oraclecloud.com.)
</Callout>
5. Click **Submit**.

<Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1786431074683-yblfr6dsohd-integrating-oracle-sales-fusion-service__02-click-submit.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" width="800" height="600" alt="Click Submit" caption=" " />
6. Scrut will redirect you to the Oracle Sales & Fusion Service authorization page. You’ll be prompted to sign into your Oracle Sales & Fusion Service account if you haven’t done so already.
7. Click **Connect to Oracle Sales & Fusion Service** to continue.
8. Once authorization is done, you’ll see the success notification and the **Connected** status indicator.

## Synchronization Details

- **Initial Sync**: Upon completing the integration, Scrut will start syncing user data from Oracle Sales & Fusion Service. This process typically takes a few hours to complete. Check the Audit Log on the Oracle Sales & Fusion Service integration page in Scrut to review the status.
- **Recurring Sync**: After this, Scrut performs the sync once **every 24 hours** to fetch incremental data such as new users, role changes, and permission updates.
- **Manual Sync**: You can also click the **Sync Now** button to trigger an immediate data refresh.

## Data Fetched from Oracle Sales & Fusion Service

Scrut fetches the following access-related data points from Oracle Sales & Fusion Service:

- User Name
- User Email
- User Role

## Viewing Oracle Sales & Fusion Service Data in Scrut

To view the data fetched from your Oracle Sales & Fusion Service account, go to the **People → Access → Overview** tab. On this page, you can see the user details for your Oracle Sales & Fusion Service account.

You can also create an Access Review (or edit an existing one) and include the Oracle Sales & Fusion Service application in scope. Refer to this guide for step-by-step instructions on [creating an access review](https://help.scrut.io/docs/creating-a-new-access-review).

Follow these steps to create a new access review for Oracle Sales & Fusion Service:

1. Go to **People → Access** and click **Create New**.
2. Enter Review Details: Name, Description, Owner, and Review Days.
3. In step 2, search for and select Oracle Sales & Fusion Service in the Applications in Scope.
4. Select **Reviewer** and **Approver**.
5. Review the details and click **Save Review**.

From the Access Review page, click Oracle Sales & Fusion Service to view user details in Scrut.
