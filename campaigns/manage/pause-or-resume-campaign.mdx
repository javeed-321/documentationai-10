---
title: "Pause or resume campaign"
description: "Pause an active campaign or resume a paused campaign from the All Campaigns view."
---

Pause an active campaign or resume a paused campaign. Pausing a campaign changes it status from "Active" to Paused." Resuming a campaign changes its status from "Paused" to "Active."

## Pause

1. From the All Campaigns view of the dashboard, click <img src="https://docs.sessionm.com/Resources/Images/Assets/actionbutton.png" alt="Action button" /> in the table row that contains the campaign that you want to pause. Then select **Pause**.
2. Click **Pause** on the confirmation dialog. The campaign's status changes to "Paused."

## Resume

1. If you want to resume a paused campaign, click <img src="https://docs.sessionm.com/Resources/Images/Assets/actionbutton.png" alt="Action button" /> and select **Resume**.
2. Click **Resume** to confirm the change. The campaign's status changes from "Paused" to "Active."
