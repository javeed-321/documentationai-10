Delete a specific behavior or message defined for a campaign. Note that a deletion cannot be undone.

## Steps

1. From a campaign's main page, locate the customer behavior or message you want to delete.
2. Click the **delete icon** that displays for the behavior or message. A dialog opens asking you to confirm the deletion.
3. Click **Yes**. The behavior or message is removed from the campaign's main page.
