End a campaign.

## About this task

Ending a campaign transitions a campaign from the "Active" or "Paused" state to "Completed."

## Steps

1. From the All Campaigns view of the dashboard, click <img src="https://docs.sessionm.com/Resources/Images/Assets/actionbutton.png" alt="Action button" /> in the table row that contains the campaign that you want to end. Then select **End**.
2. Click **End** on the confirmation dialog to confirm the change. The status changes to "Completed."

A campaign in the "Completed" state can be relaunched or deleted.
