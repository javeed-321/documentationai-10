---
title: "Review campaign"
description: "Review a Draft, Active or Pending campaign using the summary panel on the campaign's main page."
---

Review a campaign for with a status of "Draft," "Active," or "Pending."

## Steps

1. Select More Options on the campaign's main page.
2. Then click **Review Campaign**. A summary panel slides into view, detailing campaign characteristics that include runtime, target audience and advanced settings as well as any specified messages or behaviors.
3. Close the summary panel by clicking either its close (X) button or a screen location anywhere outside of the panel.
