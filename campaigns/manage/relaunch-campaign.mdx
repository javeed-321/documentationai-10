Relaunch a completed campaign.

## About this task

- Limit the use of this operation to testing on staging machines. Do not use the relaunch operation to re-create a common campaign type, which can lead to inaccurate reporting. Define a new campaign from scratch instead.

- A setting in the SessionM platform limits the number of days that you can relaunch a campaign after it has completed. The range is from 20 to 100 days, with a default of 60. Contact your SessionM Customer Success team if you want to change this setting.

## Steps

1. From the All Campaigns view of the dashboard, click <img src="https://docs.sessionm.com/Resources/Images/Assets/actionbutton.png" alt="Action button" /> in the table row that contains the campaign that you want to relaunch. Then select **Relaunch**.
2. Click **Relaunch** on the confirmation dialog. The status changes to "Pending."
3. Click the <img src="https://docs.sessionm.com/Resources/Images/Assets/actionbutton.png" alt="Action button" /> button and select **Launch**.
4. Click **Launch** on the confirmation dialog. The status changes to "Active."
