Delete a campaign.

## About this task

Deleting a campaign transitions a "Completed," "Active," or "Draft" campaign to "Deleted." Deleting a campaign removes all aspects of that campaign from the SessionM platform.

## Steps

1. From the All Campaigns view of the dashboard, click <img src="https://docs.sessionm.com/Resources/Images/Assets/actionbutton.png" alt="Action button" /> in the table row that contains the campaign that you want to delete. Then select **Delete**.
2. Click **Delete** on the confirmation dialog. The campaign's status changes to "Deleted." The campaign is no longer listed on the All Campaigns view.
