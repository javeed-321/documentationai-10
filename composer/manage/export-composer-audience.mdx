---
title: "Export Composer audience"
description: "Export a Composer-built audience via the Audiences module once it is in the Active state."
---

Composer-built audiences are exported via the Audiences module.

## Steps

1. Navigate to the Audiences module and locate the Composer-built audience in the All Audiences view. Confirm the audience status is **Active**.
2. Follow the steps found here to export: [Export audience or snapshot](/audiences/manage/export-audience-or-snapshot)
