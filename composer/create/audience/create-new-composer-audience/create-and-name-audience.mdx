---

Create and name a new audience.

## Steps

1. From the Composer module main page, click **Create Audience** in the top right corner.

   <Image src="https://docs.sessionm.com/Resources/Images/Procedures/comp_create_name_1.png" width="780" height="222" alt="Create Audience button in the top right corner of the Composer module main page" caption="" />

2. In the pop-up, provide the audience with a unique name, click **Create**, and you will be taken to the Audience Builder page.

3. Be sure to save your audience upon initial creation by clicking the **Save** button.

<Image src="https://docs.sessionm.com/Resources/Images/Procedures/comp_create_name_2.png" width="780" height="190" alt="Save button on the Audience Builder page" caption="" />

**Note**: You can always change the name of the audience if needed by clicking the pencil icon at the top of the Audience Builder page. Just remember to **Save** the audience after making any edits.

<Image src="https://docs.sessionm.com/Resources/Images/Procedures/comp_create_name3.png" alt="Pencil icon for renaming the audience at the top of the Audience Builder page" width="785" height="239" caption="" />
