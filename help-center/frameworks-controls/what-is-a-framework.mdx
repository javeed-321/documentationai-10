---
title: "What is a framework, control, and requirement?"
description: "The three building blocks of compliance in Scrut, and how they fit together."
---

These three terms come up constantly. They are worth ten seconds each.

## What is a framework?

- A compliance standard your organization follows, such as SOC 2, ISO 27001, or GDPR.

## What is a requirement?

- A specific clause or criterion inside a framework. A framework is made up of many requirements.

## What is a control?

- A policy, process, or tool that satisfies one or more requirements. Controls are where the actual work lives.

<Callout kind="tip">
  One control can satisfy requirements across several frameworks at once, which is what keeps multi-framework programs manageable.
</Callout>
