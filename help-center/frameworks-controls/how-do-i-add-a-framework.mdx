---
title: "How do I add a framework?"
description: "Add a standard to your workspace and start tracking progress against it."
---

Adding a framework creates its full requirement set in your workspace.

## How do I add one?

1. Go to **Frameworks** and click **Add framework**.
2. Choose the standard you want to track.
3. Confirm the scope, then click **Add**.

## Can I remove a framework later?

- Yes. Removing a framework stops progress tracking but does not delete your controls or evidence.

## What if my standard is not listed?

- You can build a custom framework and define its requirements yourself.
