---
title: "How do I invite teammates?"
description: "Add people to your workspace and give them the right level of access."
---

Compliance is a team activity. Most tasks need an owner who is not you.

## How do I add someone?

1. Go to **Settings → Team**.
2. Click **Invite**, and enter their work email address.
3. Choose a role, then send the invitation.

## What roles are available?

- **Admin** — full access, including settings and billing.
- **Member** — can complete assigned work.
- **Auditor** — read-only access for external reviewers.
