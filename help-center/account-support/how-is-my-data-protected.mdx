---
title: "How is my data protected?"
description: "How Scrut handles encryption, access, and the data it collects from integrations."
---

Scrut holds compliance evidence, so the platform is built to the standards it helps you meet.

## Is data encrypted?

- Data is encrypted in transit and at rest.

## What does Scrut collect from integrations?

- Only the metadata needed to evaluate a control — configuration and status, not the contents of your systems.

## Who at Scrut can see my data?

- Access is restricted and logged. Support staff access a workspace only with permission.
