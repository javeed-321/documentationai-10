---
title: "How do I contact support?"
description: "How to reach the team and what to include so the first reply is a useful one."
---

Reach support from inside the app, and include enough detail to skip a round trip.

## What should I include?

- What you were trying to do.
- What happened instead, with the exact wording of any error.
- The affected framework, control, policy, or integration by name.
- Roughly when it started.

## How quickly will I hear back?

- Response times depend on your plan. Urgent issues affecting an active audit should be flagged as such.

<Callout kind="tip">
  A screenshot of the whole browser window, including the URL bar, usually saves a round trip.
</Callout>
