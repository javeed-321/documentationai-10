description: "The categories of tools Scrut integrates with and what each one contributes."

Integrations collect evidence automatically so you are not gathering screenshots by hand.

## Which categories are supported?

- **Cloud providers** — infrastructure configuration and access.
- **Identity providers** — users, groups, and access reviews.
- **HR systems** — onboarding, offboarding, and training records.
- **Version control and project management** — change management evidence.
- **Vulnerability scanners** — findings and remediation status.

## How long does setup take?

- Most integrations connect in a few minutes using OAuth. Cloud providers may need a role or service account.
