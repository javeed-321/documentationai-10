---
title: "My integration stopped working. What should I check?"
description: "A short checklist for the most common causes of a disconnected integration."
---

Work through these in order. The first two explain most cases.

## Have the credentials expired?

- Tokens and secrets expire. Reconnect the integration to issue fresh credentials.

## Did someone change permissions?

- If the connected account lost access, or was offboarded, evidence collection stops. Reconnect with an account that still has access.

## Is the scope still correct?

- If projects or accounts were renamed or removed, update the scope configuration.

## Still stuck?

- Disconnect and reconnect the integration. If it fails again, contact support with the integration name and the time it last succeeded.

<Callout kind="alert">
  Reconnecting does not delete previously collected evidence. It only restores future collection.
</Callout>
