---
title: "What is the onboarding checklist?"
description: "What the checklist covers and how to work through it with your team."
---

The onboarding checklist is the fastest route from an empty workspace to a working compliance program.

## What does the checklist cover?

- Confirming your organization profile.
- Selecting the frameworks you intend to certify against.
- Connecting your first integrations so evidence starts flowing.
- Inviting teammates and assigning owners.

## Can more than one person work through it?

- Yes. Tasks can be assigned to different owners and completed in any order.
- Progress is shared, so the whole team sees the same state.

## What happens when I finish?

- The compliance dashboard begins reporting live posture.
- Outstanding work moves into the Task Center.

<Callout kind="info">
  You do not have to finish onboarding in one sitting. Progress is saved as you go.
</Callout>
