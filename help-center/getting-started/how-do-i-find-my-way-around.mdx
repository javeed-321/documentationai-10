title: "How do I find my way around Scrut?"
description: "A tour of the main areas of the platform and what each one is for."
---

Most day-to-day work happens in four places.

## What are the main areas?

- **Dashboard** — your current compliance posture at a glance.
- **Task Center** — everything assigned to you or your team.
- **Frameworks** — the standards you are working towards.
- **Integrations** — the tools connected to Scrut.

## How do I search?

- Use the search box in the top bar to jump to any framework, control, or policy.
- Search matches names and descriptions.
