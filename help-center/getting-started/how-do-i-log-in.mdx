---
title: "How do I log in to Scrut?"
description: "Sign in for the first time, and what to do if the login link does not arrive."
---

Scrut runs in your browser. There is nothing to install.

## How do I sign in for the first time?

1. Open the invite email from Scrut and click **Accept invitation**.
2. Enter your work email address on the login screen.
3. Choose **Sign in with SSO** or request a one-time passcode.

## I did not get the invite email. What now?

- Check your spam and quarantine folders.
- Confirm your administrator used your work address, not a personal one.
- Ask an admin to resend the invitation from **Settings → Team**.

## Which browsers are supported?

- Recent versions of Chrome, Edge, Firefox, and Safari are supported.
- Scrut is designed for desktop. Small screens are not recommended for setup work.

<Callout kind="tip">
  Bookmark your workspace URL after the first login so you can jump straight back in.
</Callout>
