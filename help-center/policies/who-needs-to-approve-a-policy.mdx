---
title: "Who needs to approve a policy?"
description: "How the approval workflow runs, and what to do when it stalls."
---

A policy moves from draft to published through an approval chain you define.

## How many approvers can I add?

- Add up to five approvers per policy, and set the order in which they review.

## What if an approver is unavailable?

- Send a reminder from the approval timeline.
- An admin can reassign the approval to someone else.

## Who can publish?

- Admins and the policy assignee can publish once every approver has signed off.
