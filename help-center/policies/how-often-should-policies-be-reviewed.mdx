---
title: "How often should policies be reviewed?"
description: "Setting a review cycle and keeping policies from going stale."
---

Most frameworks expect an annual review at minimum.

## How do I set a review cycle?

- Open the policy, then set a review frequency in its settings. Scrut notifies the owner when a review is due.

## What happens if a review is missed?

- The policy is flagged as overdue and the linked controls reflect that until the review is completed.

<Callout kind="info">
  Reviewing a policy and changing nothing is still a valid review. Record it so the audit trail stays continuous.
</Callout>
