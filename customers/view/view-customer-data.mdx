---
title: "View customer data"
description: "Access any tab in the Customers module to view a customer's activity, profile, cards, campaigns, offers, notes and privacy data."
---

Viewing the details for a customer account is critical to ensuring that the customer is successfully engaging with your program. The profile header at the top of the module provides a quick view of the customer. But, it's the tabs below that give an in-depth look at the data - across a variety of domains - associated with a specific customer.

## About this task

Accessing a tab to view its data is the same for every tab in the Customers Module. The simple procedure below applies to the following tabs, along with their child sub-tabs:

- Activity
- Profile
- Stored Value Cards
- Campaigns
- Offers
- Notes
- Data Privacy
- Recommendations

Note that the set of tabs shown above reflect a typical implementation of the platform. Your organization's implementation may differ.

## Steps

1. Search and locate the customer. For more information, see [Search Customers](/customers/view/search-customer-profiles).
2. Click the tab with the type of data you want to view.
