title: "View offers issued to customer"
description: "View the offers issued, redeemed, expired or still available for a customer on the Offers tab."
---

View the offers that have been issued to a customer. Information is shown for offers that have been issued, redeemed, have expired or are still available.

## About this task

The Offers tab is added to a customer profile when your company uses the Campaigns module.

## Steps

1. Search and locate the customer. See [Search Customers](/customers/view/search-customer-profiles) for more information.
2. Click the **Offers** tab to see offer information (described in detail below).

## Detailed information

### Issued tab

Lists all offers issued to this customer (also known as the user's wallet). Use the quick action button on the right to assist a customer:

- **Mark as Redeemed** - Redeems the offer on behalf of the customer. For ACCR, as agents are entering the FLXM code when booking on behalf of the customer, this is not used.
- **Revoke and Exclude** - Revokes the offer from the user's wallet and returns it to inventory.
- **Change Redemption Dates** - Overrides the redemption dates of an offer, allowing it to be redeemed after the original redemption date has expired.
- **Update User Offer FlexField** - Adds meta data to the user offer if needed by another system. For ACCR, it will be unlikely that this will be used.

### Redeemed tab

Lists all offers redeemed by this customer. Use the quick action button on the right to return a redeemed item to the Issued state, allowing it to be shown again in the user's wallet. For example, if the offer was consumed for a transaction but the transaction was later canceled.

### Expired tab

Lists all offers issued to a customer that have expired due to the rules of the offer.

### Available tab

Lists all active offers in the Offers module that can be issued to a customer. Use the quick action button on the right to issue an offer to a user, making it immediately available in the user's wallet and listed on the Issued tab.
