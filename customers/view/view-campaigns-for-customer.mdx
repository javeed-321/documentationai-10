---
title: "View campaigns for customer"
description: "View the active and completed campaigns a customer is participating in or is eligible to participate in."
---

View the active campaigns in which a customer is currently participating or is eligible to participate, or the completed campaigns in which the customer had previously participated.

## About this task

The Campaigns tab is added to a customer profile when your company uses the Campaigns module. For an active and completed campaigns, the following information is shown:

- Status
- Name
- Campaign Type
- Campaign Start Date
- Campaign End Date

## Steps

1. Search and locate the customer. See [Search Customers](/customers/view/search-customer-profiles) for more information.
2. Click the **Campaigns** tab.
