---
title: "Overview"
description: "APIs that connect a third-party point of sale or ecommerce platform to the SessionM Platform."
---

Point of Sale (POS) objects support connecting a 3rd party point of sale or ecommerce platform to the SessionM Platform.

The APIs allow 3rd parties to programmatically send transaction data and associate transactions to known members of a loyalty program. The APIs enable those using such a system the ability to do the following:

- Look up a customer.
- Retrieve a customer's offers or loyalty information such as their current point balances.
- Manage offers with operations for locking, unlocking, redeeming, and, in some cases, reinstating a specific offer on a customer's behalf after it has been redeemed.
- Send a transaction.

Note that these APIs are specifically for point of sale and ecommerce use cases and should be used in place of existing SessionM Platform APIs for any integration of this type, unless special cases merit a custom solution.
