## 1. Overview

AlgoShack offers an Enterprise model for large-scale enterprise or business. An enterprise user account is associated with large-scale business and enterprise that has six or more users. As an enterprise user, you can upgrade to the paid version or renew your existing version by clicking on Upgrade/Renew button in the My Subscription page. To upgrade or renew your service, navigate to the My Subscription page by clicking your Profile name in the bottom-left corner of the Main screen and click the Upgrade/Renew button. As an enterprise user, you will be able to generate unlimited test cases and unlimited projects, with a default 1000 MB storage limit. You can contact sales to customize the storage.
