Everything you need to go from a fresh signup to a working workspace with your first integration connected.

<Callout kind="tip" collapsed="false">
  New here? Work through the quickstart below in order. Each step takes a few minutes and unblocks the next one.
</Callout>

## Quickstart

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create your workspace" icon="building" title-type="p">
    Sign up, name your workspace, and pick a region. Region cannot be changed later, so choose the one closest to your team.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Invite teammates" icon="users" title-type="p">
    Open **Workspace settings → Members → Invite**. Enter one email per line and assign a role before sending. Invites expire after 7 days.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Connect an integration" icon="plug" title-type="p">
    Go to **Developer settings → Apps** and create an OAuth app, or install a partner connector from the directory.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Roles

| Role   | Billing      | Members    | Projects          |
| ------ | ------------ | ---------- | ----------------- |
| Owner  | Full control | Manage all | Full access       |
| Admin  | View only    | Manage all | Full access       |
| Member | No access    | View only  | Assigned projects |

## Billing and plans

Upgrades take effect immediately and are prorated against the remainder of the current cycle. Downgrades apply at the start of the next cycle, so paid features stay available until then.

Invoices are available under **Billing → Invoices** as downloadable PDFs. The billing contact receives a copy by email when each invoice is issued.

## Developer integrations

<ExpandableGroup>
  <Expandable title="OAuth apps" default-open="false">
    Create an app to receive a client ID and secret. At least one redirect URI must be registered before an authorization flow can complete.
  </Expandable>

  <Expandable title="Webhooks" default-open="false">
    Webhooks are registered per project. Endpoints must be HTTPS and return a 2xx status within 5 seconds. Failed deliveries retry with exponential backoff for 24 hours.
  </Expandable>

  <Expandable title="API keys" default-open="false">
    Keys can be rotated without downtime. Create the replacement key, deploy it, confirm traffic has moved, then revoke the old one.
  </Expandable>
</ExpandableGroup>

<Callout kind="alert" collapsed="false">
  Never commit API keys or client secrets to version control. Store them in your platform's secret manager.
</Callout>
