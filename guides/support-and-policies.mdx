## 1. Overview

A Pro user account is associated with a small companies or businesses that has up to five users. These users are entitled to additional features and benefits. However, Pro users do not have access to enterprise-specific deployment options. As a Pro user, you can choose to:

Upgrade to an Enterprise model if you need enterprise-specific deployment options. Upgrade to the paid version of the Pro model. Renew your existing Pro model subscription. To upgrade or renew, navigate to the My Subscription page by clicking your profile name in the bottom-left corner of the main screen, then click the Upgrade/Renew button. As a pro user, you will be able to generate unlimited test cases and unlimited projects, with a 1000 MB limit, by default.
