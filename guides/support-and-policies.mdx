Where to go when something breaks, and what the platform commits to on data and compliance.

## Troubleshooting

<ExpandableGroup>
  <Expandable title="Login and session issues">
    Clear the session cookie and retry in a private window. This resolves most cases where a stale token is being replayed.

    If the problem persists across browsers, the account may be locked after repeated failed attempts. Locks clear automatically after 30 minutes.
  </Expandable>

  <Expandable title="Slow page loads">
    Check the status page first. When status is green, disable browser extensions one at a time. Content blockers are the most common cause.

    Persistent slowness on a single project usually points at a workflow with a wait-for-all merge holding runs open.
  </Expandable>

  <Expandable title="Webhook deliveries failing">
    Confirm the endpoint is HTTPS, returns 2xx, and responds within 5 seconds. Deliveries retry with exponential backoff for 24 hours before being dropped.
  </Expandable>
</ExpandableGroup>

## Contacting support

Include your workspace ID and the request ID shown in the error banner. Those two values let support find the exact trace without a back and forth.

| Plan | Channel | First response |
|---|---|---|
| Free | Community forum | Best effort |
| Team | Email | 1 business day |
| Enterprise | Email and shared channel | 4 business hours |

## Data handling

Deleted workspaces are purged from primary storage within 30 days and from backups within 90 days. Until purge completes, a workspace can be restored by support.

## Privacy and GDPR

Data subject requests are handled through the data request form and acknowledged within 72 hours. Export and erasure are both supported.

Regional data residency is available on Enterprise, with EU and US regions selectable at workspace creation. Region cannot be changed after creation.
